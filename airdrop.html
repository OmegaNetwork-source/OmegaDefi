How to Use Omega Airdrop Tool

1

Connect Your Wallet

Choose between MetaMask wallet connection or private key input. Ensure you have sufficient tokens for distribution.

2

Enter Token Details

Provide the ERC-20 token contract address and the amount you want to send to each wallet.

3

Add Recipient Addresses

Enter wallet addresses manually (one per line or comma-separated) or upload a CSV file with addresses.

4

Execute Airdrop

Review all details carefully, then click "Start Airdrop" to begin the distribution process.

⚠️ Important: Always double-check addresses and amounts before confirming transactions.
